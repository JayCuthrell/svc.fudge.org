# 2026 company and logo verification

This manifest covers only the companies added in this update. Verification was performed on **2026-10-25**.

## Inclusion standard

- The company URL returned HTTP 200 and was confirmed as the company’s official site.
- The logo is the company’s own SVG artwork linked from or embedded in that official site.
- SVGs containing embedded raster images were rejected; no PNG-to-SVG wrappers, generated monograms, or third-party logo-service assets are used.
- Official SVG artwork was serialized as standalone, minified SVG and visually rechecked; no shapes, logos, or brand elements were replaced. Elveo’s two official CSS colors (`#fff` and `#a2151c`) were made explicit so its artwork renders outside the website stylesheet.
- No pre-existing company entry, URL, description, logo, category, or subcategory was edited or removed. This update makes no corporate-status corrections.
